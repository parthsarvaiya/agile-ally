Jira Connection

Data is fetched through the installed eng-metrics-jira connector (no base URL or token needed here: the connector holds them server-side). Each team's Board ID is set at onboarding and editable on the Team Velocity History tab.

Data Management

Backup / Restore

One JSON with everything: teams, members, sprints, PTO, scheduler, and all flow-analytics data (never the Jira token).
Auto-sync

Flow benchmark thresholds

Defaults follow common industry reference ranges. Adjust to your team's context - a platform team and a data team have different healthy ranges.

Chart annotations

Mark team changes, incidents, or holidays. They appear as dated markers on the lead and cycle time scatterplots.
